Terms of use

These terms are the rules for using AmigoFit. By creating your account or profile you accept them. Please read them: they're short.

What AmigoFit is

AmigoFit is a free app, offered by AmigoFitApp, to find training partners: publish workouts in parks and gyms, join other people's, chat with them and rate workouts and parks.

AmigoFit only puts people in touch. We don't organize, supervise or lead the workouts, and we are not coaches or health professionals.

Who can use it

You must be at least 18. AmigoFit is for meeting people you don't know, so it isn't allowed for minors. If we find that an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it.

You can't use AmigoFit if we have removed your account before for breaking these terms.

Your account

Use your real name or a name that doesn't impersonate anyone, and a photo of yourself or none. One account per person.

You are responsible for what is done with your account: don't share your password. If you think someone has accessed it, change your password and email us.

Training and meeting other people

You train at your own risk. Choose workouts suited to your level and health; if in doubt, ask a doctor first. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.

We don't verify users' identities. Always meet in public places, in daylight and with other people around, tell someone you trust where you'll be, and don't share your address or private details until you know the person.

If someone makes you feel unsafe, leave, block them and report them from the app. If there is danger, call the emergency number (112 in Europe).
