Privacy policy

At AmigoFit we only use the data needed for you to find training partners. Here we explain what it is, where it is stored, who can see it and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible

The person responsible for your data is AmigoFitApp. For any questions or to exercise your rights, email us at amigofit.support@gmail.com.

What data we use

Account: your email and a password (stored encrypted; nobody can read it), or your Google account if you choose "Continue with Google". In that case Google sends us your name, your email, your Google photo and an identifier of your Google account; we use the email and the identifier for your account and don't use your Google photo in the app.

Profile: your name, your training level, your phone's time zone (for the per-day and per-week workout limits) and, if you want, a profile photo and your nationality (shown as a flag next to your name).

Activity: the workouts you publish or join (place, day, time and participants), the ratings you give and receive (also those of parks), and your badges and achievements.

Chat: your chat requests (who wants to talk to whom) and the text messages you send and receive. Messages are end-to-end encrypted: only your phone and the other person's can read them. The people you block are stored only on your phone.

Location: only while you use the app and if you give permission, to center the map and show you nearby parks and workouts. We don't keep a history of your locations. When you check in at a workout to verify your attendance, we store once the position at that moment, its accuracy and the distance to the meeting point, so that nobody can mark workouts as completed without going. No other user sees that position.

Settings: for example, when you want to receive your workout reminders (only on your phone).

Where it is stored and who sees it

Your account, profile, workouts, check-ins, ratings and chat requests are stored on our server, hosted by Supabase (Supabase Inc.) on servers in London (United Kingdom), a country with an EU adequacy decision. Supabase acts as our processor and doesn't use your data for anything else.

Other signed-in users can see your profile (name, photo, level and nationality), public workouts with their participants, whether someone checked in (not where they were) and ratings. Private workouts created from a chat are only visible to the invited people.

Chats: your messages are stored encrypted only on your phone. To reach the other person they pass encrypted through our server, which can't read them and deletes them as soon as their phone receives them (and after 14 days at most). On the phones, messages are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Like any internet service, our provider logs technical connection data, such as the IP address, for a short time to keep the service secure.

Third-party services

Sign in with Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) verifies your account and sends us the data listed above. Google's privacy policy also applies.

Maps: to show the map we use Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone. These services receive the map area you are viewing and technical connection data, such as your IP address.

Parks and gyms: the parks, workout areas and gyms of every country in the app are included in it (OpenStreetMap data), so to show them we don't send your location or the map area to any service.

Park photos: when you open a park whose photo is on Wikimedia Commons, the app downloads it from Wikimedia (which receives your IP address, like any website). For other parks, our server may look up Google Places with the park's name and location; that lookup includes no data about you.

Email: reports are sent through an email provider (Resend) acting as our processor. Your account emails (for example, to change your password) are sent by our server provider.

Advertising: the app shows Google AdMob ads (Google Ireland Ltd.) on some screens. The first time, we ask for your consent with Google's form: if you accept, Google may use a device identifier, your IP address and technical data to show you personalized ads and measure them; if not, you'll see less personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy. On iPhone you also decide whether to allow tracking. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We don't sell your data or use analytics tools. Ads never receive your name, your email, your chats or your exact location.

Device permissions

Location (while using the app): for the map of nearby workouts and for the attendance check-in.

Photos and camera: only to choose or take your profile photo.

Notifications: for your workout reminders. They are scheduled on your own device.

You can withdraw any permission in your phone's settings at any time.

Safety and moderation

You can only chat with someone if they accept your request. You can block anyone so they can't write to you, and report users or messages from their profile or from the chat.

Reports are emailed by the app to our team (amigofit.support@gmail.com) with your username as the sender; your email address is not shared. They include your name and user ID, those of the reported person, the reason, your comments and, if you report from a chat, the reported message and the last 20 messages of that conversation (you send them from your phone, because we can't read chats). Our server only forwards the report and does not keep it; it only counts how many you sent that day, for the daily limit, and deletes that counter the next day. We use reports only to review the case and remove anyone who breaks the rules, and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

How long we keep data

Your account and data are kept until you delete your account. You can do it whenever you want from Profile → Delete my account: we immediately delete your account, profile and photo, the workouts you organize, your participations, check-ins, ratings, chat requests and undelivered messages. Messages already on another person's phone are deleted there automatically after 14 days.

If you can't use the app, ask us to delete your account by emailing amigofit.support@gmail.com from your account's email address; we'll do it within 30 days at most.

Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone (chats, blocks and settings), but not your account on the server.

Your rights

You can access and correct your data from your profile, and delete it by deleting your account. You also have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to object to processing, to restrict it and to data portability, by emailing us at amigofit.support@gmail.com.

If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Minors

AmigoFit is not intended for children under 14. If you are under 14, don't use the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, if the change is important, we'll let you know in the app.
